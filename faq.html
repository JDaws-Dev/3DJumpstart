Equipment & Supplies

Not required, but helpful! Students learn how to operate a 3D printer in class. Having one at home lets them practice what they've learned and print their own designs.
Students learn professional 3D modeling and how to operate a 3D printer. The goal is to teach them the skills to create and print their own projects, not to print everything for them.
We exclusively recommend and support Bambu Lab printers. The Bambu Lab A1 is perfect for beginners - it's reliable, easy to use, and produces professional-quality prints. It's the same brand we use in class, so students will already know how to operate it.
