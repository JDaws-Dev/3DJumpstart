## Image Creative Requirements

### Technical Specs:
- **Format:** 1200 x 628 pixels (1.91:1 ratio) for Feed
- **Format:** 1080 x 1080 pixels (1:1 ratio) for Instagram/optimal mobile
- **File type:** JPG or PNG
- **File size:** Under 30MB
- **Text on image:** Keep under 20% of image area (use Facebook's Text Overlay Tool to check)

### What to Photograph:

#### Option A: Student Success (BEST)
- Photo of actual student at computer designing
- Student holding their finished 3D print with proud expression
- Make sure student's face is visible and excited
- Good lighting, clean background
- Show computer screen with Fusion360 visible

#### Option B: Student Work Showcase
- Grid of 6-9 student projects on white background
- Use your existing project images: cat vase, fidget spinner, ring, bottle, automata
- Add subtle text overlay: "What Students Create"
- Keep text minimal and in corners

#### Option C: Instructor + Student
- You helping a student at computer
- Both looking at screen, student pointing
- Shows personal attention, small class environment
- Builds trust (parents see who's teaching)

#### Option D: Before/After Concept
- Left side: Kid on phone/gaming
- Right side: Same kid designing/holding 3D print
- Visual metaphor for "screen time to skill time"
- Use arrow or "→" between images

### Text Overlay Guidelines:
- **Use sparingly** (20% max)
- **Placement:** Top or bottom third only
- **Font:** Bold, sans-serif (Arial Black, Montserrat, Bebas)
- **Examples:**
  - "Starting Oct 25 • Suwanee"
  - "After School Classes"
  - "Ages 9-17 • $40/week"
  - "Professional 3D Design"

### Colors to Use:
- Your brand orange (#ea580c) for text overlays
- White text with orange background
- Or orange text with white background
- High contrast for mobile readability

## Testing Strategy

### Week 1 (Oct 15-18): Audience Validation

**Run:** Ad #1 (Screen Time) to your configured audience

**Why:** Test if your targeting is correct before testing multiple creatives

**Monitor:**
- **CTR (Click-Through Rate):** Should be 1.5%+
- **CPC (Cost Per Click):** Should be $1-2
- **CPM (Cost Per 1000 Impressions):** Should be $15-25 in Suwanee

**Daily check:** Are you getting 10-15 clicks per day?

**If YES:** Audience is good, move to creative testing
**If NO:** Audience may be too narrow or creative needs work

---

### Week 2 (Oct 19-23): Creative Testing + Scale

**Run:** 3 ads simultaneously to same audience
- Ad #1: Screen Time
- Ad #3: Results Focused
- Ad #5: Direct Value

**Split budget:** $7/day per ad

**After 2 days, evaluate:**

| Metric | What It Means | Action |
|--------|--------------|---------|
| CTR > 2% | Ad is engaging | Keep running |
| CTR 1-2% | Ad is okay | Monitor |
| CTR < 1% | Ad is weak | Pause it |
| CPC < $1.50 | Efficient | Increase budget |
| CPC > $2.50 | Too expensive | Pause or adjust |

**Scale the winner:**
- Turn off worst performer
- Increase best performer to $20/day
- Keep 2nd place at $10/day (backup)

---

### Final Push (Oct 21-23): Urgency

**Update winning ad:**
- Change primary text to emphasize urgency:
  - "Only 2 days left to enroll for Oct 25 start"
  - "Last chance - classes start Friday"

**Increase budget:**
- If ad is performing well, boost to $30/day for final 3 days
- Focus budget on best-performing placement

## Ad Copy Best Practices

✅ **DO:**
- Lead with transformation ("Turn screen time into skills")
- Mention specific location ("Suwanee")
- State price upfront ("$40/week")

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue: "Ad Not Approved"

**Reasons:**
- Too much text on image (>20%)
- Claims that can't be verified ("Best class in Georgia")
- Use of "you/your" in image text
- Targeting minors directly

**Solution:**
- Reduce text on image
- Change "you" to "your child"
- Avoid superlatives
- Target parents (35-50), not kids

### Issue: "Low Reach"

**Reasons:**
- Audience too small
- Budget too low
- Poor relevance score

**Solution:**
- Expand radius to 20 miles
- Remove some detailed targeting
- Improve ad creative (higher CTR = more reach)

### Issue: "High CPC (>$3)"

**Reasons:**
- Audience too competitive
- Poor ad creative
- Wrong placement

**Solution:**
- Test different audience interests
- Change image/headline
- Switch to manual placements, exclude Audience Network

### Issue: "Lots of Clicks, Zero Enrollments"

**Reasons:**
- Landing page is slow
- Price shock (ad didn't mention $40)
- Enrollment process too complicated
- Wrong audience clicking

**Solution:**
- Test mobile page speed (should load <3 seconds)
- Make sure price is in ad copy
- Simplify enrollment flow
- Add negative keywords or exclude certain interests
