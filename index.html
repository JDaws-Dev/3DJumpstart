Meet Your Instructor

Hi, I'm Jeremiah

Teacher • Former Disney Producer • Professional Maker

I teach Film and Creative Technology at Artios Academies of Sugar Hill, where I pioneered the 3D design curriculum that's impacted 50+ students. Before teaching, I was a video producer for Disney in LA and Orlando, and ran my own machining business designing custom equipment for Marvel films.

I learned CAD, machining, 3D printing, welding, and electronics during the pandemic—and discovered my passion for teaching students to bring their ideas to life. There's nothing better than watching a student hold something they designed in their own hands.

I have degrees in Film (Georgia State) and Machining (Lanier Tech). I'm the "ultimate fun uncle" to my 9 nieces and nephews (built them a pirate-themed treehouse!), grew up in Gwinnett County, and love being back home teaching the next generation of makers.

"The most rewarding part? When students voluntarily spend hours on their projects because they're genuinely excited about what they're creating. That's when I know they've caught the maker bug."

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents want to know

What does my child need to bring?

Just themselves! We provide all computers, software, and equipment. Students should bring a set of Imperial (inch) dial calipers which can be purchased on Amazon for about $15. Everything else is included.

What if my child doesn't like the first class?

100% money-back guarantee! If your child doesn't love their first class, we'll refund you in full. No questions asked. We're confident they'll be excited to come back, but if it's not the right fit, you risk nothing.

Is there a contract or long-term commitment?

No contracts! Pay $40 for the first week to reserve your spot. After that, you're only charged $40 for weeks your child attends. Stop anytime with no cancellation fees.

How does billing work?

You'll be charged $40 for each class your child attends. Charges are processed after each class (Saturday students charged on Saturday, Monday students charged on Monday). Miss a week? No charge. You only pay for what you use.

What's the attendance policy for reserving a spot?

When you enroll, you're reserving a spot in a limited class (max 10 students). To keep your reserved spot, your child should attend regularly. After 2 consecutive missed classes, your spot may be offered to students on the waitlist. We're flexible for illnesses, family emergencies, and other extenuating circumstances—just let us know! We simply need to prioritize active students when spots are limited.

How many students per class?

Maximum 10 students per class. Small group sizes ensure every student gets personal attention and help when they need it.

Does my child need prior experience?

No! This is Level 1 - perfect for beginners. We start with the basics and build up to advanced techniques. If your child can use a mouse and keyboard, they can succeed in this class.

What will my child actually learn?

Students learn professional skills used by real engineers: precision measuring using calipers (down to .001" accuracy), 3D modeling with Autodesk Fusion 360 (professional CAD software used by engineers and product designers worldwide), how to design their own creations from scratch, understanding different filament types and their applications, and how to operate Bambu Labs 3D printers including slicing, settings, and troubleshooting. These aren't toy skills—they're career-building skills.

Where exactly are classes held?

Classes are held in North Suwanee in a safe, accessible facility with parking. The exact address will be sent via email after enrollment to maintain a secure learning environment.

What about holidays and school breaks?

We follow Gwinnett County's school calendar for major holidays. No class = no charge. You'll receive the full schedule after enrollment.

Are all class times guaranteed to start?

Classes launch based on enrollment. If your preferred time slot doesn't have enough students by the start date, we'll contact you personally to either switch to an available time or wait until your preferred slot fills. Full refunds available if neither option works for you.
