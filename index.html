Meet Your Instructor

Hi, I'm Jeremiah

I help students go from "I can't figure this out" to "Look what I made!"

For the past few years, I've been teaching 3D modeling and printing to middle and high school students. I've seen the transformation that happens when students learn to create their own designs instead of just downloading files.
